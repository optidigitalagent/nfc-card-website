Kyiv*Type Sans Black by Dmytro Rastvortsev (2019–2020).
Created for Kyiv city identification with Projector, Dmytro Bulanov Creative Büro and Banda Agency.

The designer states that Kyiv*Type is free for commercial and non-commercial use under CC BY-ND 4.0. The font file must not be decompiled or modified. This site embeds an unmodified KyivType Sans Black WOFF2 for selected headings.
