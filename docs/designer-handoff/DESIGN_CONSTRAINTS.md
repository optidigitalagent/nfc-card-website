# Design scope and invariants

The current graphite/silver NFC CARD interface is the accepted editable baseline. The owner and human designer will discuss creative direction separately. This transfer does not create new design approval or authorize publication.

The designer can refine composition, responsive grids, typography using licensed fonts, spacing, gallery treatment, product cards, tabs, banners, illustration, honest media frames, microinteractions and focus states. Preserve NFC CARD/Antonov Digital brand identity unless the owner directly authorizes a change. Discuss major new art direction with the human designer/owner; do not treat legacy CSS classes as a permanent ban on approved visual changes.

The following are functional or factual invariants:

- Six discoverable physical products; four home priorities in approved order; separate personal-design service; all 9 legacy offer IDs/URLs and 2 unavailable drafts.
- Current prices, market rules, variants, product purpose, characteristics, existing copy and FAQ. No invented Restaurant Mini dimensions/price, Universal availability, promotions, guarantees, Google partnership or customer results.
- Real in-place four-tab interaction; all static content, accessibility and SEO. Do not replace long text with fewer marketing sentences or anchor scrolling.
- UA/EN/PL meaning, stable links, page-equivalent locale switching, search/filter, gallery, configuration, cart and forms.
- One cart→one enquiry, server-authoritative quotes, partial/quote distinctions, idempotency and historical receipts. No payment checkout.
- V36 email proposal and its distinct request-scoped consent; existing v1/v2 API shapes; protected gateway/CRM boundary.
- Original media provenance,27 placeholders,30 approved omissions, Restaurant Mini on home and two separate HOLD slots. Fake client photos, reviews, badges and outcomes are prohibited.

Run all three locales at 320/360/390/393/430/768/1024/1280/1440px, native 200%zoom, keyboard-only navigation and reduced motion. Check first/middle/footer paint, visible CTA text,44px primary targets, gallery, tab focus/Arrow/Home/End behavior and no-JS reading. A green geometry assertion alone does not prove readable composition.

Use references for usability principles only. Do not copy reviewscard.com code, photos, discounts, claims, proof or font files. New media needs source/rights and truthful claim-role labels. Owner-provided public business assets may be reused only for their correct role.

Backend, API, database, CRM, publication, privacy approval and business decisions require separate scope. Do not change `.github/workflows`, default branch, deployed site, Railway or sender settings for a design iteration. Submit a reviewed PR to the handoff branch.
